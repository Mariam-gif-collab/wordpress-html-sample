Sherwani Estate & Builders was established in 2006 by Anas Khan Sherwani. The company was formed to develop well designed and competitively priced real estate in Pakistan.

Over the past decade we have been consistent in our objective of contributing our share to the growth and development of Pakistan’s economy as well as improving a lot of individual and corporate clients through investment in real estate. Sherwani Estate & Builders is pre-eminent focused property company with a large, well balanced portfolio comprised of residential, commercial and mixed-used properties. It is renowned for its impressive on-going development of residential properties in Chinar Bagh.
